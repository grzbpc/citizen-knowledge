### The story

At least 23 people were injured, some requiring CPR, when a crush developed at Ghana Party in the Park in Barnet on Saturday evening. A small fire near the entrance led organisers to close the gate temporarily; when it reopened, dozens of people surged forward and were trampled. Police and paramedics treated the injured, including using megaphones to tell arriving festival-goers to go home. Organisers said "the decision to close the festival was made solely on the grounds of health and safety."

### The reframe

The statement organisers gave answers a question nobody asked. Nobody is disputing why the event was cancelled after the crush; cancelling was obviously correct. The question the statement does not touch is why a gate closure at a busy entrance, at a festival organisers were responsible for managing, produced a crowd dense enough to trample people once it reopened. That is a question about crowd management before the fire, not about the decision made after it.

Answering the wrong question convincingly, while the right one goes unaddressed, is its own small performance. It offers a decision that sounds responsible in place of an account that would actually explain what happened.
