### The story

Frances Connolly, 60, and her husband Paddy, of Hartlepool, set up the PFC Trust after their 2019 EuroMillions win of £115 million, and have given half of it to good causes in the north east of England. The trust has bought caravans so young carers can take a break, funded loneliness projects, delivered emergency food parcels and installed defibrillators at local sports clubs. Frances said: "I think it's in mankind to share when you get to a level where you've got enough." Of her own circumstances she said: "We have the same life, we just live it bigger."

### The reframe

Half of £115 million is £57.5 million, and the other half is still more than most people will earn in several lifetimes of work. Frances Connolly says as much herself when she says they "just live it bigger". The gift is large in every absolute sense, and the cost to the giver is, by her own description, small. That is the unusual feature of this story. Most generosity that gets praised is the large number. Most generosity that costs something is a small one, given by someone with little margin, and it rarely gets a headline of any size.
