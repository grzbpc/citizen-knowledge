### The story

Rhys Wynne-Jones, 34, had lived on the streets for a decade until he walked into St Mary's Church in Penzance and played Bohemian Rhapsody. A video of the performance has been viewed more than 10,000 times. Known as The Nightchurch Pianist, he has helped raise £38,000 for charity and now lives in a Coastline Housing home in west Cornwall.

### The reframe

This is a good outcome, and the report does not say what was tried in the decade before. What it records is that the home arrived after a video had been seen by more than 10,000 people. That is not a route any outreach programme can offer, and it is not one most of the people it works with will have. For them the sequence the manuscript describes, contact, temporary accommodation, short-term stabilisation, exit, return, carries on without a camera.
