### The story

An upskirting website targeting women and girls has been taken down following Metro's reporting. The site claimed to hold 400 videos of victims who were secretly filmed as they walked around London. Searches for the site now return a "Page Not Found" result, although it may have been geo-blocked for the UK or moved to a different server. Ofcom confirmed it did not take the site down. Last Friday, Metro reported that Ofcom was "actively reviewing" the site under the Online Safety Act, and that the Metropolitan Police had not confirmed whether it was investigating.

### The reframe

On the screen, the problem has gone. A search that once returned the site now returns an error page, and anyone reading only that would conclude that something was done. The report is careful to say what can and cannot be known. The site may have been blocked for UK visitors only. It may have moved to another server. Ofcom, the regulator with the power under the Online Safety Act, says it did not remove it. Nobody has said who did.

That leaves an outcome with no author. If the operator chose to withdraw the site, the rule has not been enforced, because nobody has been made to do anything. If it is still online elsewhere, the videos have not gone, and the women in them are exactly where they were a week ago. The only certain change is that a URL no longer resolves in the place where a journalist looked.

The manuscript describes the stage at which enforcement stops aiming at correction and starts aiming at the appearance of control. This is an unusual version of it, because no institution has claimed the result. The appearance has arrived by itself. A page that cannot be found looks like compliance, and the process that was said to be under way, a review by the regulator and no confirmed police investigation, can continue as before without having to produce anything. Metro's reporting changed the position of one website. Whether the law about upskirting changed anything at all is a question that nothing in this week's report answers.
