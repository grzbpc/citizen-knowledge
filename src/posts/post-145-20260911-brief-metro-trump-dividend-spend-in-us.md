### The story

Donald Trump has vowed to give every adult American citizen a $5,000 payout if the Republicans win both chambers of Congress in November's midterm elections. At a party convention he said: "If the Republicans win the House of Representatives and the United States Senate, both of them, I will issue a dividend to every adult citizen in the United States of $5,000." He added a condition: "We don't want you going to Canada to spend the money. We don't want you going to China, to Germany. You got to spend the money in the United States of America." Metro reports that it is unclear how the government would monitor where the money is spent, and that with more than 270 million adults the payout would cost $1.3 trillion. The Democrats called the pledge an "empty promise".

### The reframe

Whether the promise could ever be kept is a separate matter. The condition is the interesting part, because a rule about where money is spent can only be enforced by seeing how it is spent. A rule that every dollar must stay in one country is only as strong as the records of where each one went. The question Metro raises, how it would be monitored, has an obvious answer, which is the same transaction data that the rest of modern life already generates. It would not need new surveillance. It would need only that someone be allowed to read it.
