### The story

Passengers whose trains are cancelled will automatically be able to use another operator to complete their journey free of charge from this weekend. Currently passengers have to wait for operators to agree ticket acceptance, leading to delays and cancelled journeys. Rail minister Lord Hendy said: "No one should be left stranded because of a fragmented railway when their journey is disrupted." Operators who have signed up to the agreement include Gatwick Express, Great Northern, LNER and Southern. Alex Robertson of the Transport Focus watchdog said: "This is a welcome change that we have long called for."

### The reframe

The change is welcome, and the interesting part is the length of time it took. Until now a passenger with a valid ticket and a cancelled train had to wait while the companies involved agreed between themselves whether to carry them. No single company was being unreasonable. Each had its own ticket and its own rules, and each could say that the matter was not its decision alone. The cost fell on the one party who had done everything correctly and had nobody to ask. The Transport Focus watchdog says it has "long called for" the change, and the minister's own word for the cause is "fragmented". The agreement covers the operators who have signed up, of which the report names four.
