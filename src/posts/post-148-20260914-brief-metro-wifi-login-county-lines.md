### The story

A drug dealer found hiding in a wardrobe has been jailed for 12 years after police traced him through an accidental public Wi-Fi login. "How did you find me?" Joel Louis, 34, of Wembley, London, asked. The county lines boss normally used a VPN to mask his location.

### The reframe

The man in this report did what privacy advice tells people to do. He used a VPN, which exists to stop exactly this kind of tracing. What defeated it was one ordinary lapse, a single login to a public network, and the lapse was enough to connect the rest. The story is about a criminal and the outcome is not a cause for sympathy. It is a clear demonstration of the principle, which applies whether the person concerned is guilty or not: protection built from careful separation lasts only until the first moment two things touch.
