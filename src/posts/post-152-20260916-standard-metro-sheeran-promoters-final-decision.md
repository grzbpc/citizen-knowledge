### The story

Ed Sheeran has denied being "complicit" in the decision to axe support act Macklemore from his Loop tour for saying "free Palestine" on stage at New Jersey's MetLife Stadium. The decision was taken by promoters, and Sheeran lost nearly 100,000 Instagram followers amid a backlash. He said: "Just because I choose not to speak publicly, it doesn't mean I don't care. I am appalled by the conflict between Israel and Palestine. The suffering and pain caused on both sides is a human tragedy." He explained that "venues communicated they would pull the shows if he was still supporting", which was "based on how Macklemore conveyed part of his message, rather than on everything that was said". He made it clear that axing Macklemore was "the promoter's final decision, not mine".

### The reframe

This entry takes no position on the conflict that Macklemore was talking about. It is about how the decision was made and who owns it.

Sheeran's account has three links. The venues said they would pull the shows. The promoter, in response, made the final decision. The artist, whose tour it is, says he did not make it. Each statement is plausible on its own, and each is consistent with the others. Together they describe a decision that was reached without anyone deciding it. The venues set a condition, the promoter met the condition, and the artist, who would have been the one to refuse, was not in the position of refusing.

The manuscript's account of inconsistent enforcement is that people are sanctioned less for what they did than for how exposed they were when they did it. Sheeran's explanation is that the decision rested on how Macklemore conveyed part of his message, rather than on everything he said. No rule of the tour is cited in the report, and no process by which that judgement was reached. What is described is a consequence that arrived by way of other people's contracts, applied to the person with the least say in them.

The line that carries the most weight is the last one. "The promoter's final decision, not mine" is an honest description of who made the call. It is also a description of how the arrangement works: a decision that affects the artist's own tour, the artist's own audience and the artist's own name can be made by someone else, in response to someone else, and still land on him. Sheeran has lost followers over something he says he did not decide, and Macklemore lost the slot. The party who made the choice, according to the report, is the promoter, and the report records no statement from it.
