### The story

Police have backed a ban on balaclavas in Nottingham city centre after the new rules came into force. Supt Chris Pearson of Nottinghamshire Police said the coverings were a "substantial problem", adding: "We've seen footage of people wearing balaclavas, throwing fireworks, chucking things off the roofs of buildings." Nottingham is one of the first areas to bring in a ban. Penalties include fines and bans from the city centre.

### The reframe

The conduct the superintendent describes is throwing fireworks and objects off roofs. The rule covers what people wear while doing it. There is a case for that, since a covered face makes identification harder and gives officers something to act on before the thing is thrown. It is also a rule about appearance, and appearance is the part of the problem that is easy to see. The measure of whether it works will be fewer fireworks and fewer objects, which is a different figure from the number of fines.
