### The story

Robert Kraft claims Ed Sheeran asked him to make a $2 million (£1.5 million) charity donation before Macklemore announced his $1 million pledge. Macklemore, removed from the US leg of Sheeran's Loop Tour over his speech on stage calling for a "Free Palestine", said he would donate his "entire" $1 million earnings from the tour to groups helping Palestinians, and challenged Kraft "to match my donation". Kraft, owner of the New England Patriots, then said: "Before Macklemore challenged me to match his donation, Ed called me and asked me to commit $2m to match his donation to aid in the region." Kraft and Sheeran are yet to reveal details of any donations.

### The reframe

Two sums have been mentioned and no payment has been described. Macklemore has pledged his tour earnings, Kraft says he was asked to commit $2 million, and Kraft and Sheeran have yet to give any details of donations. What exists in the report is a sequence of pledges, a challenge and a disagreement about who asked first. The order appears to matter to the people involved. The money itself, and the groups it would reach, are the part the report does not describe. Whatever each man intends, the announcement is already doing its work, and the donation has not been reported.
