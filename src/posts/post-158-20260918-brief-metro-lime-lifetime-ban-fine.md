### The story

A rider who put a finger up at a woman who remonstrated with him has been banned from using Lime bikes for life. The e-biker was caught on camera riding on the wrong side of the road before mounting a pavement to avoid colliding with an oncoming bus. CCTV showed him weave around pedestrians in London's Fleet Street, narrowly missing the woman. When she challenged him, he made the rude gesture. She alerted police, who worked with Lime to find him. He has since paid a £50 fine for careless cycling and apologised for his conduct following the incident in July.

### The reframe

The conduct was careless and the woman was right to report it. What stands out is how the identification worked. The camera showed a rider and a gesture. The company supplied the rest, because the bike was on its system and the ride was on an account. The sanction that followed is also the one with the longer reach. A £50 fine is paid and finished with. A lifetime ban from a service is imposed by the company that holds the records, through a process the report does not describe, and it has no end date.
