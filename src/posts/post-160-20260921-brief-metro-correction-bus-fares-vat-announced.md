### The story

In its corrections column, Metro said an article in Thursday's paper "stated that Andy Burnham had cut bus fares and VAT on fuel bills since becoming prime minister". It added: "In fact, these measures had been announced but have not yet taken effect." In July, Metro had reported Mr Burnham's announcement of a £2 bus fare cap, with the cost said to be met by re-prioritising money freed up by scrapping the digital ID scheme.

### The reframe

The correction is short and honest, and the error is a useful one. A newspaper whose business is to separate announcements from events described an announcement as an event, and it took until the following Monday to correct it. That is how well an announcement works. The sentence "has cut bus fares" reads naturally because the announcement was loud, specific and repeated, and the cut, which has not yet happened, would have been quiet. The manuscript's point is that completion is slow and unremarkable while announcement is immediate and visible, so a reader, or a reporter, takes the second for the first.
