### The story

A 9ft high memorial to Michael Collins, brother of the self-styled "traveller king", has appeared next to a £200,000 headstone for his sibling, bare-knuckle boxer Willy Collins, in Sheffield's Shiregreen Cemetery. Willy died in 2020, aged 49, and Michael in 2024, aged 51. Locals say the giant headstones make the cemetery look like "haunted crazy golf". The city council has advised the family to submit a retrospective application for the shrines.

### The reframe

Nothing in the report suggests the family acted with any intention other than to remember their relatives, and this entry takes no view on the memorials. It looks at the remedy. A rule that permission comes before building is turned, by the council's advice, into a form that can be completed afterwards. That is a practical and ordinary thing for a council to say. It also shows how a rule can remain in force on paper while the order of events is reversed, which is a position nobody who has applied for permission in advance and waited will have been offered.
