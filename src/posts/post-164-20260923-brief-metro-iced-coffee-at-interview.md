### The story

Recruiters warn that bringing a cup of coffee to a job interview may calm the nerves but could cost the candidate the role. A debate on TikTok highlighted a divide between Gen Z jobseekers, who say carrying a coffee, iced or warm, is completely normal, and millennial and Gen X hirers, who say it makes a candidate look overly casual or indifferent, with points deducted for noisy ice and frivolous straws. Ashlea Fisher, a hirer at Swindon agency IRecruit4, told Metro that an interview is a chance to spend quality time with the hiring manager, and recalled a candidate with a coffee in one hand and a bag in the other for whom the handshake was awkward. Katrina Collier, who has written books on hiring, said iced coffee can be distracting and messy.

### The reframe

Nobody in the story claims there is a rule. The recruiters say it is a matter of impression, the candidates say it is normal, and both are describing the same cup. What the story shows is that a candidate can lose points for something that appears in no instructions and is judged by someone who does not say so. The only way to learn it is to be marked down once, or to know someone who was.
