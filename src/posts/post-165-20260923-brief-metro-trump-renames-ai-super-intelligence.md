### The story

In his speech at the United Nations in New York, Donald Trump sought to reassure those who fear that artificial intelligence will be the death of us all. He announced that he is officially changing the name to "Super Intelligence", and that all US government documents will refer to "SI" rather than "AI" from this point forward. He said: "The use of the word artificial makes intelligence fake, it makes it sound fake and it is not fake. It's actually amazing." He said the fears are "perpetuated by the Radical Left".

### The reframe

Whether the technology is safe or not is a separate argument and this entry does not take it up. What can be said is that a name can be changed the same day it is announced, and that the fears the speech was addressing were about something other than the name. The announcement is complete the moment it is spoken, whether or not anything it describes has moved.
