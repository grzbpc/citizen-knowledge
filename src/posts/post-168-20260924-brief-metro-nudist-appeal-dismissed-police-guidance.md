### The story

A nudist arrested after strolling naked past an off-duty policeman has had his appeal dismissed at the High Court. Neil Cox was convicted of disorderly or threatening behaviour by Crewe magistrates and fined £800 after Detective Chief Inspector Mark Whittaker spotted him on the Middlewood Way walking trail near Macclesfield, carrying a copy of the College of Policing's naturism guidance. Two women also saw him on the 6.30am walk in 2023. Dame Victoria Sharp found the original court was "plainly entitled" to find he was disorderly.

### The reframe

The court has decided and this entry does not argue with it. The point is the document in his hand. He carried the published guidance, in the expectation that it would explain his position to anyone who stopped him, and it did not settle the matter. Guidance says what the police should consider. The offence turns on whether other people were caused alarm or distress, and that was decided afterwards, on the facts, by a court.
