### The story

Nearly a fifth of households are in "hygiene poverty" and half of these have even gone without toilet roll, a poll shows. Deodorant and nappies also have to be skipped, according to the charity In Kind Direct, which said families who recently felt financially safe now struggle to buy the basics. Metro's photograph showed essentials being packed at a support hub.

### The reframe

The figure comes from a poll commissioned by a charity that supplies these goods, so it should be read as that charity's measure of a need it is already working to meet. It is still a plain description of what is being given up. Toilet roll is not a luxury that a household can defer without cost. The manuscript's concern is with where the burden of such a gap falls. When the response to a shortage is a hub where goods are packed and handed out, the shortage is being met, and the question of who is responsible for it moves quietly out of view. The poll counts the households. It does not say who was expected to prevent it.
