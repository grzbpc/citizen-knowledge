### The story

A supermarket tomato soup costing 59p has been crowned better than Heinz in a blind taste test. A Which? panel of 77 people compared tinned cream of tomato soups from Tesco, Sainsbury's, Aldi and Asda with big-name versions by Heinz and Baxters. Tesco and Sainsbury's took joint first place, scoring 78% each, and Asda's 59p tin scored 77%. Heinz, at £1.70 for 400g, scored 68% and landed in the bottom three, with more than a third saying the flavour was "too weak". Baxters, at £2.05, was the most expensive and scored lowest on 58%.

### The reframe

A panel of 77 is a small group, and soup is a matter of taste, so this is not a ruling on anything. It is a clear instance of something the manuscript says about prices in general. A price is often a record of how a thing is positioned, and not of what it is. A blind test removes the label and leaves the product, and here the order of the prices and the order of the scores turned out to be almost opposite. Anyone who chose the more expensive tin was paying for something other than the soup, and may well have found it worth paying for.
