### The story

Residents in Barnham, Suffolk, are to hold a "Piddington-style referendum" over plans to house 930 asylum seekers at a nearby RAF base, Metro reports. The vote is symbolic and will decide whether the village votes to "leave" the UK over the proposal. John Bauer of the parish council called village asylum protests a "powerful movement".

### The reframe

The report does not say what the plans involve or who decided them, and this entry takes no side on the policy. It notes only the form of the response. A vote described as symbolic is not presented as able to change the decision. What it can do is show feeling in a way that is counted and reported. The manuscript describes politics that increasingly rewards visible responsiveness, and it is a fair question whether a ballot with no power is a way of being heard or of being seen to be heard.
