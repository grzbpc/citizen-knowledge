### The story

The number of complaints about broadband fell in the last quarter, the regulator Ofcom says. BT, EE, TalkTalk and Vodafone were Britain's most complained about broadband providers, with each receiving seven complaints per 100,000 customers. Virgin Media received the fewest, with four per 100,000 customers, followed by Plusnet and Sky, which both received five. The biggest reasons for complaints about broadband, landline and mobile services from April to June were faults, service quality and getting services connected.

### The reframe

The report gives rates, not causes, so this entry cannot say why complaints fell. The figures are a useful measure and nothing here suggests they are wrong. What stands out is the scale of the unit. Seven complaints per 100,000 customers is a small number, and the "most complained about" providers are the ones at the top of a scale that runs from four to seven. The manuscript's concern with measurement is that an indicator can come to stand for the thing it was built to track. A customer whose line is faulty and who has not complained does not appear in the count.
