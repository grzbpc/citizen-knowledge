### The story

Daniesha Charles was 15 and earning £7 an hour at the Royal Thurso Hotel in the Scottish Highlands when she was subjected to what an employment tribunal described as a "tirade of abuse", Metro reports. She was left in tears after the hotel's chef and general manager shouted and swore at her over dropping a portion of apple crumble. The tribunal in Edinburgh ruled she had been discriminated against because of her age, sex, race and disability. It heard she "suffered in silence" because she feared losing her job, and she left the hotel after working there between May and June 2024. Employment judge Joseph d'Inverno found the conduct violated her dignity and created a "degrading" environment. The employer was ordered to pay £11,500 in compensation, and a further £516 for not giving her written particulars of employment.

### The reframe

The tribunal's findings are as Metro reports them. The entry notes the sequence. The protections that applied to a 15-year-old at work existed throughout, and she said nothing because speaking could cost her the job. The ruling came after she had left, and the report says she is now 18. The manuscript describes how a rule that is uneven in practice works through exposure. Here the exposure fell on the person with the least power, and the remedy arrived once she was no longer there.
