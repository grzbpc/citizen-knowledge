### The story

Flavio Bolsonaro, Brazil's defeated presidential candidate, has fled to the US with his family to avoid a corruption probe, Metro reports. The senator, 45, his wife and two daughters arrived in Miami on a plane from Ecuador. Donald Trump confirmed he had granted visas. Mr Bolsonaro is the son of the jailed former president Jair, and lost to Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva in Sunday's vote. On Monday, a court ordered him to surrender his passport amid an inquiry into alleged corruption and fraud. He went to the US, via Argentina, the next day.

### The reframe

The corruption and fraud allegations are allegations, and Metro's report carries no response from Mr Bolsonaro. The entry does not take a view on them. It notes the sequence: a court ordered the passport surrendered on Monday, and the next day he was travelling, and then landed in the US with visas the American president said he had granted. The manuscript says that consequences follow position as much as conduct. What a court can do depends on where a person can go, and that depends partly on who is willing to receive them.
