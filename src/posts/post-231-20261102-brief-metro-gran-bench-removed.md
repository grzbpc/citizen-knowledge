### The story

Metro reports that Katharine Hoskyns, 89, has been left devastated after Westminster City Council removed a bench from the garden outside her home in St John's Wood, north-west London, following a neighbour's complaint. She created the garden more than 25 years ago, to tend to her tomatoes, geraniums and herbs. The council cited "anti-social behaviour" and "drug use", and said the bench had drawn "repeated complaints". Ms Hoskyns, a retired teacher, says there is no anti-social behaviour, and that she loves the bench because people come up and talk to her. The council has offered to return it to her back garden.

### The reframe

The council's reason and Ms Hoskyns's account cannot both be right, and Metro reports neither the content of the complaints nor any police involvement, so the entry does not say which is. It notes what is established: a garden tended for more than 25 years, a bench taken away after a complaint, and a settlement that would return the bench to a place where nobody would see it from the street. Metro does not say how long the bench had stood, so the entry cannot say it was ignored until now. It records only that the removal followed the complaint, and that whether the bench was tolerated until a neighbour objected is the question Metro's report leaves open.
